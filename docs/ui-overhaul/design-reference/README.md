# Design reference

These images are the "designed dashboard" Kai refers to. They are exported from the logo and theme concept board (https://claude.ai/artifact/J2hWGvcufwTo3LgLKnB5mY), and they show the intended look rather than the final layout.

- `app-preview-steel.png`: a mock of the app in Steel. It shows the look to copy:
  - see-through, blurred panels over the map with a hairline border;
  - soft glow on the primary button and the timeline playhead;
  - the Steel accent on the selected Story/Scientist button and on "Play the story";
  - the orange and blue data colours left untouched.
- `logo-system.png`: the final logo as an app icon, horizontal, stacked and on white.
- `steel-palette.png`: the Steel colours with their hex values.

Where the mock and the brief disagree, follow the brief. In particular:
- **The navigation is not at the top.** The mock has a top bar. The brief (section 5) moves navigation to a dock at the bottom of the screen, with the timeline bar just above it.
- **"Explore" and "About" are placeholders.** They are not real features, so don't add them.
- **The panel is not the real panel.** The mock's story card is a simplified stand-in. The real side panel keeps every item in the brief's section 1.
- **The map is fake.** It is a drawn background with a sample deformation blob, not real data.
